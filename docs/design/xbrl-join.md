## 2. The join (in scope regardless of the FD-DEC 016 choice)

**Source, per filing:**
1. **Inline XBRL in the cached primary document** (132 filings). The facts come from exactly that accession, include
   dimensions, and need no new requests.
2. **The company-facts API** for the 12 non-inline 10-Ks: 11 requests, through the same client (declared
   User-Agent, rate limit, cache). Non-dimensional facts only. That limit is stated wherever those filings appear.

Each fact keeps its source (`inline` or `companyfacts`), so the two paths can be checked against each other.

**Extractor (`filing_delta/xbrl/`, pure: bytes in, facts out, no I/O):** `ix:nonFraction` → concept, value (applying
`scale`, `sign`, and the `ixt:` formats including dash-as-zero and parentheses), unit, decimals, period (instant or
start/end), dimensions, and context id. Hidden facts (`ix:hidden`) count; text blocks don't.

**Period alignment.** This is the part most likely to go wrong silently:
- A filing's own period comes from `dei:DocumentPeriodEndDate` and `dei:DocumentFiscalPeriodFocus`. It is never
  taken from the company-facts `fy`/`fp` fields, which describe the *filing*, so a comparative row from 2018
  carries the 2019 filing's `fy`.
- Durations are classed as quarter, year-to-date or annual by length, with a tolerance for 52/53-week years. Coca-Cola's
  Q2 2026 ends 2026-07-03 and its Q2 2025 ends 2025-06-27; Walmart's fiscal 2025 ends 2025-01-31; Apple's ends in
  September.
- Each fact is tagged `current` (the filing's own period) or `comparative`.
- Amendments: the corpus excludes 10-K/A and 10-Q/A filings, and company-facts rows from amendments are dropped by
  accession.
- **Restated comparatives** (the same concept and period with a different value in a later filing) are surfaced as a
  fact pair. They are not resolved or hidden.

**Headline set shown as context:** revenue (the first present of `Revenues`,
`RevenueFromContractWithCustomerExcludingAssessedTax`, `SalesRevenueNet`, recorded per filing), net income,
operating income, diluted EPS, total assets, cash, and operating cash flow. Non-dimensional, current period. Some
filers don't tag some of these (no `OperatingIncomeLoss` at Pfizer, JPMorgan or Exxon); that shows as "not
tagged", never as zero.

**Facts are never narrative.** A `Fact` is its own type. It never enters section text, the diff, or the M6
retrieval index as a citable span. Every rendering carries its provenance: "XBRL-tagged value from the 10-K's
audited financial statements" or "…from the 10-Q's unaudited interim statements". Tests enforce both.

**Verification (the gate).** `docs/xbrl-report.md`, generated by a command:
- Per-filing coverage: facts extracted, current period resolved, headline concepts present or not tagged.
- **A cross-check of the two independent sources.** For every filing in both, compare inline values to company-facts
  values on the same concept, period and accession. The result is a published agreement rate, with each
  disagreement listed.
- Golden tests on hand-picked fixtures (small XBRL excerpts in `tests/fixtures/`): a 52/53-week quarter, a January
  fiscal year end, a scaled negative value, a dash-as-zero, a restated comparative.

## 3. FD-DEC 016: how far to go beyond context

| | A. Context only | **B. Consolidated headline claims, no model** | C. Model-extracted claims |
|---|---|---|---|
| What it does | Shows the headline facts beside MD&A. No claim checking. | Finds MD&A sentences stating a consolidated headline metric, using a written lexicon for revenue, net income, operating income, operating cash flow and diluted EPS. Compares each to the same filing's current-period, non-dimensional fact at the stated precision. | A model extracts every numeric claim (subject, metric, period, GAAP or not). Deterministic lookup follows, including segment facts. |
| Outcomes | — | `matches` · `no matching tagged value` · `non-GAAP wording` · `not checked` (segment, other period, or no lexicon hit) | same, wider coverage |
| Coverage | — | on the order of a few hundred claims; segment claims are `not checked` | most claims, including segments |
| Model calls | none | none (consistent with the deterministic-first design) | yes, a new stage with its own hand labels |
| Your labeling | none | about 100 sampled claims on the keyboard page, roughly 30 min: is the extraction right, and is the outcome right? | about 150+ claims, plus segment-mapping judgment |
| Risk | adds little beyond the README limit | narrow, but every outcome is auditable | segment-to-member mapping is judgment; more to score and explain |

**Recommendation: B.** It turns FD-DEC 016's concern into a measured number with no new model stage. A wrongly
reported mismatch would read as an accusation of misstatement, so the outcome is worded `no matching tagged value`,
never "differs" or "incorrect". Any claim that isn't clearly about the consolidated total for the filing's own
period goes to `not checked` rather than being forced into a verdict. The published figures are the matcher's
precision on `matches` and on `no matching tagged value`, against your labels, and the share of claims it covers.
Segment claims (option C) go to the backlog with this survey as the evidence for scoping them.
