# Claim matcher evaluation (suggestion-assisted labels)

Matcher version `eb36301dae5d` (frozen before sampling), 10-K Item 7 and 10-Q Part I Item 2 of the locked corpus. 99 claims sampled from 1,085, stratified by the matcher's outcome and reason; labeled by Trevor **with the matcher's answer shown as a suggestion** (FD-DEC 026, `tests/golden/claims_labels.json`). Generated by `tools/score_claims.py`.

`no matching tagged value` means an amount wasn't found among the filing's tagged values at its stated precision. It is not a finding that the statement is wrong.

**These figures measure agreement with one human labeler, not accuracy.** The labels are one careful reader's judgment and can be wrong; every disagreement is listed below with its text so it can be checked. Labeling was **not blind**: the labeler saw the matcher's answer before answering, so agreement is biased upward by anchoring and is not an independent estimate of the matcher's precision. The most informative figure is the override count: where a reader looking at the evidence rejected the matcher's answer.

Not scored: C001, explained to the labeler as a worked example during labeling. Scored: 99.

**Suggestions overridden: 0 of 99** (unsure: 0). Each override is listed under Disagreements. Labeling pace: median 5 s between labels, about 28 active minutes.

**Reading this result:** with no overrides, the labels cannot separate "the matcher was right" from "the suggestion was accepted". The agreement figures below are an upper bound set by anchoring and carry little evidential weight on their own. The matcher's verification rests on its mechanics: concept, period and amount are deterministic, each rule is unit-tested and mutation-checked, and every `matches` requires the stated amount to fall within its own rounding of the filing's whole-company value.
