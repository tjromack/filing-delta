# Materiality evaluation: test set, adjudicated (secondary)

**The headline is the blind result** (`docs/materiality-eval-test.md`). This page re-scores the same classifier output (`claude-sonnet-5-5`, prompt `1fd1c7e5bb42`) after Trevor relabeled the 26 disagreements with the context the classifier saw (the restatement passages), without the classifier's label or rationale. Labels: `tests/golden/materiality_recheck.json`. Generated by `tools/score_recheck.py` (offline).

| | agreement · "material" precision (95% CI) · recall (95% CI) |
|---|---|
| blind (headline) | 89/115 (77%) · precision **0.89** (0.81–0.96) · recall **0.80** (0.68–0.89) |
| adjudicated | 96/113 (85%) · precision **0.94** (0.88–0.99) · recall **0.90** (0.83–0.96) |

Stratum-weighted, stratified bootstrap with 2,000 resamples, same seed as the blind run. Items relabeled **U** leave the scored set, as unsure human labels do in the blind run.

## What the re-check changed

- Relabeled to the classifier's label: **7** (2 of them had restatement context). Held the blind label: **17** (7 with context). Now unsure: **2**.
- **The information-gap explanation mostly fails.** The blind error analysis attributed the misses to the labeling sheet not showing restatement context. Shown that context, Trevor kept his label on 7 of the 9 items that had it, every split/merged one included. Those are a real disagreement about the guide: the classifier treats restated text as cosmetic (tie-break rule 3 plus the classifier notes), while Trevor treats a restructured passage that adds or drops disclosure as material.
- Re-check pace: median 4 s between labels (blind pass: 16 s).

## Why this is not the accuracy figure

- Only disagreements were revisited. Agreements were never re-checked, so errors where both sides were wrong the same way stay hidden, and agreement can only rise.
- The labeler knew every item on the page was a disagreement. That invites deference to the classifier even though its label was not shown.
- What it does measure is how much of the blind gap came from the labeler and the classifier seeing different context, the information gap found in the error analysis.
