# Alignment relabel: same instance, or just the same template?

These are the pairs you marked **Y** or **?** in the first pass. The question is now narrower: is the NEW paragraph **the same instance**, meaning the same subject or event, revised?

- **Y**: same instance. The same risk reworded; the same metric, segment, region or matter updated with new numbers or dates; the same paragraph expanded or shortened. *e.g. "Net sales rose 5% in 2024" → "Net sales rose 3% in 2025".*
- **T**: same *template*, different instance. The same kind of sentence about a different region, segment, product, event or fact. *e.g. "Sales in Europe rose 4%" → "Sales in Latin America rose 6%"; "In 2019 we sold business A" → "In 2020 we exited business B".*
- **N**: on a second read, not related at all.
- **?**: still can't tell.

Rule of thumb: if the NEW text appeared in the filing and the OLD one didn't, would a reader call it *new information*? If yes, it's **T** (or N). If it's the old information updated, it's **Y**.
