# Trevor's questions for the QA set (M6, FD-DEC 028)

Write **12 or more** questions you would genuinely want answered from these filings. Write them before you see the
drafted ones, so they stay in your own words. You don't need to know the answer, or even whether the filings contain
it. Some will turn out unanswerable, and that's useful too.

**What's in the corpus:** Apple, Coca-Cola, Netflix, Walmart, Pfizer, Ford, Weyco Group, Berkshire Hathaway,
JPMorgan Chase, Exxon Mobil, General Electric.
- 10-K risk factors (Item 1A) and MD&A (Item 7), for fiscal years 2018 to 2025.
- 10-Q MD&A and risk-factor updates, for quarters filed January 2025 to September 2026.

**A good question:**
- names the company and, ideally, the filing or year ("in its 2023 10-K", "in the Q2 2026 10-Q");
- asks what the filing **says**: a risk, an explanation, a figure management states, or what changed between two
  years;
- doesn't ask for advice or predictions. The set already has some of those as must-refuse tests, so you don't need
  to write them.

**A mix helps:**
- a few lookups ("What does Netflix say drove its 2024 revenue growth?");
- a few about whether a risk is mentioned ("Does Pfizer's 2021 10-K discuss COVID-19 vaccine pricing pressure?");
- one or two about change ("What new risk did Ford add between its 2022 and 2023 10-Ks?");
- one or two you suspect the filings *don't* answer.

Write one per line after the dash. Add a note after `//` if you like. Save the file and tell Claude when you're done.
