# Trevor's questions for the QA set (M6, FD-DEC 028)

Write **12 or more** questions you would genuinely want answered from these filings. Write them before you see the
drafted ones, so they stay in your own words. You don't need to know the answer, or even whether the filings contain
it. Some will turn out unanswerable, and that's useful too.

**What's in the corpus:** Apple, Coca-Cola, Netflix, Walmart, Pfizer, Ford, Weyco Group, Berkshire Hathaway,
JPMorgan Chase, Exxon Mobil, General Electric.
- 10-K risk factors (Item 1A) and MD&A (Item 7), for fiscal years 2018 to 2025.
- 10-Q MD&A and risk-factor updates, for quarters filed January 2025 to September 2026.

**A good question:**
- names the company and, ideally, the filing or year ("in its 2023 10-K", "in the Q2 2026 10-Q");
- asks what the filing **says**: a risk, an explanation, a figure management states, or what changed between two
  years;
- doesn't ask for advice or predictions. The set already has some of those as must-refuse tests, so you don't need
  to write them.

**A mix helps:**
- a few lookups ("What does Netflix say drove its 2024 revenue growth?");
- a few about whether a risk is mentioned ("Does Pfizer's 2021 10-K discuss COVID-19 vaccine pricing pressure?");
- one or two about change ("What new risk did Ford add between its 2022 and 2023 10-Ks?");
- one or two you suspect the filings *don't* answer.

Write one per line after the dash. Add a note after `//` if you like. Save the file and tell Claude when you're done.

1. - What does Netflix's 2023 10-K say about the effect of its paid-sharing rollout on membership growth?  // lookup
2. - In its fiscal 2023 10-K, what does Walmart's MD&A cite as the main driver of the change in gross profit rate?  // lookup, figure + explanation
3. - Does Coca-Cola's 2022 10-K discuss the Russia-Ukraine conflict as a risk to its operations?  // risk mentioned
4. - Does Apple's 2020 10-K risk factors section mention COVID-19 disruption to its supply chain or manufacturing partners?  // risk mentioned
5. - Does Exxon Mobil's 2019 10-K discuss climate-related litigation as a risk?  // risk mentioned
6. - How did Ford's description of electric-vehicle risks change between its 2021 and 2024 10-Ks?  // change, multi-year
7. - What new risks, if any, did JPMorgan Chase add between its 2022 and 2023 10-Ks related to the First Republic acquisition?  // change
8. - What does Berkshire Hathaway's 2025 10-K say about CEO succession and leadership transition?  // lookup, recent
9. - What does Pfizer's Q1 2026 10-Q update about drug-pricing or tariff risks compared to its 2025 10-K?  // 10-Q vs 10-K delta
10. - What does Weyco Group's 2025 10-K say about the impact of tariffs on its gross margin?  // lookup, small-cap
11. - How many iPhones did Apple sell in fiscal 2023, according to its 10-K?  // likely unanswerable, Apple stopped reporting unit sales
12. - What does GE's Q3 2026 10-Q say about aerospace order backlog?  // unanswerable, filed after the corpus cutoff
