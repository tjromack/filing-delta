## Validation

- JSON Schema (draft 2020-12, with format checks) and a re-read of every span from its section text, with each section's sha256 re-checked: **all items valid**.

## For Build 4

- Spans are sentence-level: the answer key marks the sentences that answer a question, and a gold span is a whole sentence, not a minimal phrase.
- `gold_answers` holds each labeler's short answer where the jurors agreed (two phrasings of one answer), or the human's answer where they split.
- An unanswerable item has no evidence. Its category says why: outside coverage, not in the corpus, advice or prediction, undisclosed, or (for human-written questions) `trevor`.
- The 48 model-drafted questions were drawn from passages picked by a deterministic sampler and are easier for models than the 12 human-written ones (filing-delta FD-DEC 028, 032).
