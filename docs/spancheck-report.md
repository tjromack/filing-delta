## Limits

- The key is model-built (FD-DEC 031), so these are agreement figures, not an independent measurement.
- The judge isn't calibrated on this corpus. `spancheck` asks for a human gold set before a judge is trusted; its disagreements with the lexical proxy above are where calibration would start.
- Groundedness (lexical) and citation support (lexical) are word-overlap proxies: paraphrase lowers them and a negation can fool them. That's why the judged versions are reported beside them.
- "No, but" answers (FD-DEC 033) assert an absence. A citation can support the "it does say Z" half but never the "doesn't say X" half, so support graders score them against their best-matching sentence (`claim_split`).
