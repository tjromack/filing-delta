Labeling guide (press ?)

Would you want this change flagged if you were tracking this company's risk disclosures? Judge only the text shown.

MATERIAL
The change alters what the company tells you about its risks.
new_risk
A risk, exposure or event not disclosed before
removed_risk
A disclosed risk or event no longer mentioned
severity
Likelihood or severity strengthened or weakened ("may" to "has")
realized
A hypothetical risk is now described as having happened
specifics
A new named event, country, product, counterparty or regulation
quantity
A number describing the exposure changed meaningfully
legal_status
An investigation or lawsuit opened, settled, decided or closed
COSMETIC
The same message, presented differently.
rewording
Same meaning, different words; sentences split, merged or reordered
date_rollforward
Only a year or period label changed
cross_reference
Page or note references; renamed defined terms
formatting
Typography, capitalisation, punctuation
restructure
Moved, split or merged with no change in content
Tie-breaks
"may" to "could" is cosmetic; "may" to "has" is material. A number is material if it describes the risk itself. Added or removed text is material unless it is boilerplate, a heading, or clearly restated elsewhere. Unsure is a valid answer.
