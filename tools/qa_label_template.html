How to label (press ?)
Read the question, then look for its answer in the filing text below.
The text is the full section(s) the question is about. Use the search box (or press F) to jump to words; a dashed underline marks where a drafted question started, but the answer can be anywhere.
Answerable
Select the smallest stretch of text that answers it, press S (or "Add selection"), repeat if the answer needs two places, then type a short answer in your own words and press A.
N · Not answerable
The filing text shown doesn't answer it, or it asks for advice or a prediction, or it's about a company or period that isn't here.
U · Unsure
You can't tell. Add a note if you can say why.
W · Wrong filing shown
The answer should be in this corpus, but in a filing or section that isn't shown (for example, the question compares a 10-Q with a 10-K and only 10-Qs appear). Say where in the note. This records a miss by the system's filing lookup.
R · Reject question
The question is badly worded or ambiguous. It is replaced, not scored.
